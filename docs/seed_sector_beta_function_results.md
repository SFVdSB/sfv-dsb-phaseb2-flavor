# Phase B2 v1.0.0 — exact one-loop seed-sector beta-function audit

## 1. What “suppressive” and “enhancing” mean

The effective Pati–Salam multiplicity is

\[
N_{\rm eff}=15\frac{k_4}{k_0}+3\frac{k_L}{k_0}+3\frac{k_R}{k_0}.
\]

A **suppressive** correction means the per-component adjoint kernel is smaller than the singlet kernel, so \(N_{\rm eff}<21\). An **enhancing** correction means it is larger, so \(N_{\rm eff}>21\). The flavor fit is more tolerant on the suppressive side: the inherited sub-1% interval is approximately

\[
20.49\le N_{\rm eff}\le21.16.
\]

The earlier v0.9 sign parameter was only an order marker. The exact beta functions show that there is no single sign until the physical definition of the induced kernel \(k_a\) is specified.

## 2. Explicit minimal seed model

Use one real singlet seed \(X_0\), three real adjoint blocks

\[
X_4\sim(15,1,1),\quad X_L\sim(1,3,1),\quad X_R\sim(1,1,3),
\]

and a gauge-singlet mediator coordinate \(S_2\). The relevant potential terms are

\[
V\supset\frac12M_a^2X_a^2+\mu_aS_2X_a^2+V_{O(22)}.
\]

At \(\Lambda_{\rm seed}\), \(O(22)_{\rm seed}\) imposes

\[
M_a^2=M_0^2,\qquad \mu_a=\mu_0.
\]

## 3. Exact one-loop gauge-breaking beta functions

For an adjoint block \(a=4,L,R\), the gauge parts are

\[
(16\pi^2)\beta_{M_a^2}\big|_g
=-6C_2({\rm Adj}_a)g_a^2M_a^2,
\]

\[
(16\pi^2)\beta_{\mu_a}\big|_g
=-6C_2({\rm Adj}_a)g_a^2\mu_a.
\]

The singlet receives no gauge term. Common \(O(22)\)-symmetric scalar terms cancel in block-to-singlet ratios. Consequently,

\[
\frac{d}{d\ln Q}\ln\frac{\mu_a}{M_a^2}=0
\]

at one-loop gauge order.

This is the central result: **the source-to-mass-squared ratio is protected**. The gauge interaction raises both the charged seed mass squared and its cubic source coupling by the same multiplicative factor when running from the UV toward the IR.

## 4. Why the sign depends on the induced kernel

Different UV realizations use different combinations of \(M_a\) and \(\mu_a\):

| Kernel | One-loop behavior |
|---|---|
| \(\mu_a/M_a^2\) | protected |
| \(\mu_a^2/M_a^4\) | protected |
| \(\mu_a^2/M_a^2\) | enhancing |
| \(1/M_a^2\) | suppressive |
| local zero-momentum loop mass | counterterm/scheme dependent |

Thus the beta function does not say “gauge corrections are always suppressive” or “always enhancing.” It tells us how the seed parameters run; the physical kernel then determines the sign.

### Loop-induced derivative kernel

The derivative expansion of a scalar seed bubble produces the physical scaling

\[
k_a^{(2)}\propto\frac{\mu_a^2}{M_a^2}.
\]

This is enhancing and predicts

\[
N_{\rm eff}\simeq23.73.
\]

After refitting the same four allowed absolute amplitudes, the worst flavor error is about

\[
6.5\%.
\]

Therefore this particular derivative-bubble realization fails the 1% criterion across the existing scale interval.

To retain sub-1% flavor accuracy with this kernel, the equality boundary would need to lie within approximately

\[
\frac{\Lambda_{\rm seed}}{M_{\rm match}}\lesssim1.19,
\]

rather than the current ratio \(14.8\).

### Protected auxiliary/composite kernel

A kernel depending on

\[
\frac{\mu_a}{M_a^2}
\]

or its square remains exactly at

\[
N_{\rm eff}=21
\]

at one-loop gauge order. This preserves the \(0.596\%\) four-parameter flavor result.

## 6. What fails, and what survives

The beta-function calculation rules out a specific version:

> a long-running, loop-induced derivative susceptibility \(k\propto\mu^2/M^2\) with no additional protection.

It does **not** rule out:

- an \(O(22)\) equality boundary imposed near the actual mediator matching scale;
- an auxiliary/composite construction whose physical coefficient is \(\mu/M^2\);
- a symmetry or threshold mechanism that cancels the derivative-kernel running;
- the local wall operator, rank-two flavor structure, or Pati–Salam origin of 21.

## 7. Do O(22) and compositeness both have to be used?

They solve different problems:

- \(O(22)_{\rm seed}\) explains why all 22 seed masses and common \(S_2\) couplings are equal at a boundary scale.
- The induced/composite condition explains why the \(S_1,S_2\) quadratic matrix is generated by the seed correlator rather than replaced by an arbitrary bare matrix.

Neither is logically mandatory if another mechanism performs its role. In the present construction, using both is the strongest and least arbitrary version. The beta audit further says that the composite realization must use a protected source-to-mass ratio or match close to the \(O(22)\) boundary; the unprotected derivative-bubble version is not viable over the full scale interval.

## 8. Verdict

The exact seed beta functions sharpen the project:

}
\]
